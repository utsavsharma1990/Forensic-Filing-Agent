## Diagnosis (task 1, measured first; 36 DEV cases, focus year = analysis year, as_of = analysis filing date)
Before: Beneish computable in 16/36 (fraud 8/18, clean 8/18) at the financial_metrics level. Why the other 20 were not:
* LongTermDebt missing (OSIR, OFIX, TEUM, SNCR, LPSN, LSTR, ICUI, AGX, BAND, MITK, ALOT-prior): six have NO debt line at all, so no XBRL tag exists for a zero (OSIR, LPSN, ICUI, AGX, BAND, MITK); OFIX and LSTR tag their debt with a concept outside the map (SecuredLongTermDebt, CapitalLeaseObligationsNoncurrent) or the statement row label was excluded (data-1); SNCR and TEUM have several convertible / related-party instruments.
* COGS missing (LK, CGI, SNCR, RRTS, LSTR, MTZ, MITK, BRK-B): MTZ and SNCR report `CostOfServices[ExcludingDDA]` (not in map); CGI, RRTS, LK, LSTR, MITK have no cost-of-goods line (expense-by-nature statements: GMI is undefined, nothing is invented); BRK-B has no classified balance sheet.
* Other: RIDE has no revenue (pre-revenue), SPCE has no receivables line in FY2021 (and immaterial revenue), PTRY (2004 table parse), RAH (no PPE row, 2004). SGA missing (ENRNQ, HUBG, LK, CGI ...) never blocked M: SGAI is neutral-imputed (existing rule).

## Tag validation (no new XBRL tag included): agreement of XBRL vs the filing's own statement table
Panel: 36 DEV filings (3 fiscal years each) plus 40 non-dev large filers (FY2013 and FY2018, a fixed list chosen for industry spread before any tag result): 174 filing-years, about 70 filers. Agreement = within 0.5% (or 1,000 units), counted only where the tag is the one the priority list would use. Existing tags: CostOfGoodsAndServicesSold 37/38 (22 filers), CostOfRevenue 33/35 (16), CostOfGoodsSold 14/15 (11), SG&A 72/74 (36), DepreciationDepletionAndAmortization 81/94, DepreciationAndAmortization 18/24, Depreciation 13/20, AccountsReceivableNetCurrent 97/105, ReceivablesNetCurrent 12/12, PropertyPlantAndEquipmentNet 119/122, LongTermDebtNoncurrent 63/65, LongTermDebtAndCapitalLeaseObligations 17/20. Candidates tested as additions (only where no existing tag is present): CostOfServices 6/7 (3 filers), CostOfServicesExcludingDDA 4/4 (2 filers), SecuredLongTermDebt 2/2 (1 filer), CapitalLeaseObligationsNoncurrent 2/2 (1 filer; as a co-occurring line it agrees with the table LTD row only 2/10: it is a component), ConvertibleSubordinatedDebtNoncurrent 0/1, AccountsNotesAndLoansReceivableNetCurrent 8/8 (4 filers; as a co-occurring line 3/6, EPAM notes receivable). Derived LongTermDebt = LongTermDebt - LongTermDebtCurrent: 23/29 (79%), tried and REMOVED. Rule applied: at least 10 filers and at least 90% agreement. No candidate reached 10 filers, so CANONICAL_CONCEPTS is unchanged and the statement-table fallback (with its own anchor check) carries the coverage. Several tags named in the brief (CostOfRevenue, CostOfGoodsSold, LongTermNotesPayable, ConvertibleNotesPayableNoncurrent, ...) were already in the map.
No-debt evidence rule check (evidence says "no debt" vs XBRL debt-like facts at the period end): 18 filing-years accepted; the 6 hits of my scan are not drawn debt (ALOT: credit-line capacity), OSIR 2013/2014 footnoted capital leases of USD 117k / 72k (0.1% of assets) sitting inside "Other long-term liabilities", SPCE 2021 LongTermDebt 310k is entirely current. 11 filing-years with no XBRL debt tag were not accepted by the rule (conservative).

## Not done / limits
* No new XBRL tags (none passed the 10-filer bar); no LongTermDebt derivation from total less current (79% agreement); no summing of several debt instruments; no COGS imputation for expense-by-nature statements; no receivables = 0 imputation (SPCE).
* The fallback costs one extra filing fetch and table parse per XBRL year with a missing Beneish input (cached by edgar_api).
* Footnote-only capital leases inside "Other liabilities" count as no debt (OSIR USD 72-117k).
* `comparative_by_year` is only populated for statement-table years; for XBRL years the agent re-extracts from state.xbrl_data, so a state without xbrl_data uses the originally filed prior year (flagged).
